Store Cannibalization Analysis

Understanding how nearby stores interact and what that means for network growth.

Professional work
Context
Starbucks Korea · Professional work
Role
Data Analyst · Development Planning
Period
Jan 2024 – Present

The question

Opening a new store may affect sales at existing locations. Planning needs to account for these interactions across the store network.

My approach

  • Evaluated the impact of store-to-store cannibalization on sales.
  • Examined sales patterns alongside the spatial relationships between locations.
  • Identified planning considerations to minimize performance loss at existing stores.

What came out of it

Supported store development planning with evidence about interactions between nearby locations.

Perspective

The analysis informed business recommendations; no causal effect estimate or quantitative business impact is asserted in this public overview.
