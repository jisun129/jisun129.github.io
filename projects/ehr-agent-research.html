AI Agents for Clinical Research Workflows

Exploring how AI agents and human checkpoints can support reproducible clinical data science.

In progress
Context
University of Minnesota · Capstone
Role
M.S. capstone · Advisor: Prof. Feng Xie
Period
Fall 2026 · In progress

The question

Clinical research workflows span cohort extraction, feature construction, modeling, evaluation, and external validation. My capstone is exploring where AI agents can assist this process and where human review is most useful.

Work so far

  • Reproduced an existing emergency-department modeling pipeline to understand cohort construction, data processing, and model evaluation.
  • Began reviewing research on agent-based workflows for clinical data science.
  • Am exploring human checkpoints and validation strategies as potential directions for the project.

Current stage

The project is at the research and scoping stage. The precise research question, evaluation protocol, and final results are still being developed.

What I’m exploring next

Potential directions include examining how human review affects the reliability of agent-assisted research. Emergency-department chest-pain risk stratification is an initial proposed use case.
