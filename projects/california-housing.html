California Home Price Prediction

Making sense of skewed prices and regional markets with geospatial features and time-aware model evaluation.

Completed
Context
IDX Exchange · Internship
Role
Data science internship project
Period
2026

The question

California housing prices vary sharply across regions, and a small number of expensive properties produce a long right tail. The goal was to predict sale prices while accounting for local market context and evaluating performance on a future month.

My approach

  • Transformed the sale-price target with log1p and applied preprocessing rules fitted on the training data.
  • Engineered property and location features, including property age, bed-to-bath ratio, school-district spatial joins, and trailing nearby-sales summaries.
  • Compared linear regression, decision trees, random forests, XGBoost, and LightGBM with three-fold walk-forward cross-validation.
  • Selected LightGBM using validation MdAPE, evaluated on the held-out May 2026 test month, and integrated the selected model into a Streamlit application.

What came out of it

LightGBM achieved a 9.17% median absolute percentage error on the held-out test month. The difference between MAPE and MdAPE highlights that typical error and larger prediction errors need to be considered together.

Perspective

These results describe one held-out month. The gap between cross-validation and future-month performance calls for evaluation across additional months and regional markets before making broader claims.
