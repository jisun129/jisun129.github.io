Market Segmentation & Sales Patterns

Using prediction and clustering to understand markets and identify comparable sales patterns.

Professional work
Context
OpenMate · Consulting
Role
Spatial Data Scientist & Researcher
Period
Jun 2019 – Oct 2023

The question

Markets and stores differ in customer composition and sales behavior. These differences can guide targeting and comparisons when they are represented clearly.

My approach

  • Predicted market-segment proportions using LightGBM.
  • Applied Gaussian mixture models to group markets and identify target segments.
  • Used time-series clustering to identify stores with similar sales trends.

What came out of it

Delivered client-specific analyses of market segments and sales patterns to support business decisions.

Perspective

The modeling approach was chosen around each client’s question, with the focus on useful groupings and decision-ready findings.
